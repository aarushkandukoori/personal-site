I'm Aarush Kandukoori, a Statistics, Machine Learning, and Computer Science student at Carnegie Mellon. My work spans machine learning research and building real products in healthcare and education.

I co-founded and lead **BUMP**, a patent-pending closed-loop wearable atropine pump for bradycardia. Our 2023 bench prototype pairs fingertip heart-rate sensing with an automated dosing pump, and the work is advised by Harvard Medical School faculty. I also co-founded **MoCo Innovation**, a STEM-education nonprofit that has brought hands-on programming and engineering to more than 5,000 students across underserved schools in Maryland.

My research is in machine learning and systems. Right now I work with Prof. Lin Zhong's group at Yale on [Pie](https://pie-project.org/), their programmable LLM inference engine, which I [got running on my iPhone](/posts/pie-on-my-iphone/). I'm also an undergraduate researcher in the **CMU Database Group**, working on query engines and storage, measuring whether splitting a table's columns across separate Parquet files is free at query time across the 99-query TPC-DS suite under Apache DataFusion. Before that I worked in labs at Harvard Medical School and Brigham & Women's Hospital, the Stanford Cardiovascular Institute, and the UMD MIND Lab.

Away from the work, I've played piano since I was nine, with most of my hours going to Beethoven. I also paint with oils, run, and lift.
