Toy model, illustrative only. Every turn the policy samples y = x + noise, the judge passes it if y lands within 4 of the target T, and the update moves x by k times (T minus y). The failing-turns gate comes from an idea in Frans et al. (limit feedback to turns where the policy fails). This is not their method, and none of these numbers come from their paper.
